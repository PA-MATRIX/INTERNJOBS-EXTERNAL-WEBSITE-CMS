<objective>

owning employee's Spam to that employee's Inbox, with an audit line, no auto-trust, exact matching by
`source_id` and the D32 best-effort unique match for older rows. Also the `lakera_flagged` label (LAK-04).

to a mailbox email (audit section 2: moveEmail works only on the internal UUID; nothing looks up message_id).

Audit facts re-verified in code (main d1658aa): source_id sent at inbound-email.ts:291 =
`originalMessageId ?? messageId`; stored email has `id = messageId` (uuid) and `message_id = originalMessageId`,
so `message_id = ? OR id = ?` covers both. Stored body = `parsed.html || parsed.text`; the safety preview is
`emailBody.slice(0,80)` of the same string, so a body-prefix match is valid. `audit_events` is NOT in
employeeMailboxMigrations (inserts are try/catch'd "table may not exist"), so the audit trail is a structured
console log line plus `reviewed_by` on the safety row; do not rely on audit_events.
</objective>
